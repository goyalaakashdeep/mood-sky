Support

Inner Sky lives on your iPhone. There is no account to sign into and no web dashboard. Your skies, reasons and notes stay on the device.

If something feels off — a check-in that will not save, a widget that will not update, or a question about Inner Sky Plus — email goyalaakashdeep@gmail.com. Please mention your iOS version and what you tapped last.

Common questions

Why is my forecast not showing yet? The forecast reads patterns across days, so it needs check-ins on four different days. Several check-ins on the same day count as one day.

The Lock Screen buttons open the app instead of logging. iOS needs the phone unlocked with Face ID or Touch ID before a widget button can act. Tap again after unlocking.

Restoring Inner Sky Plus. Open Settings → Inner Sky Plus → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so they come back on a new iPhone.

Deleting my data. Delete the app and every sky, note and reason goes with it. If iCloud backup was on, turn it off in Settings first to clear the backup copy.
